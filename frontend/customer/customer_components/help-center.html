Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my booking?

You can cancel your booking directly from the "Bookings" tab. Open the booking details and click "Cancel Booking" at the bottom. Please note that cancellations within 2 hours of the slot may incur a small convenience fee.

What is your refund policy?

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days to your original payment method. If you used GlossFlow Credits, the refund is instantaneous. No-shows are not eligible for refunds.

How do I reschedule a wash?

Go to your "Bookings" tab, select the active appointment, and choose "Reschedule." You can pick a new date and time from the available slots without any extra charges if done 4 hours in advance.
